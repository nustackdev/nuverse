1.2 m onto concrete, 26 drops per unit (6 faces, 12 edges, 8 corners), 5 units. Room temperature. Units powered and reporting throughout.

## Results
- 5 of 5 still report after all 26 drops.
- Unit 3: gasket shifted on drop 19 (corner, cap down). Looked fine. Failed IP67 afterwards, water in the cap after 8 min.
- Unit 5: probe tip bent 4 degrees on drop 22 (tip down). Still reads within 1% VWC of the others. Growers do drop them tip down, onto paths.
- Cell holder clip held on every unit. Cell never lost contact (checked on the trace, no resets).

## Takeaways
- Gasket needs a locating rib. 0.3 mm, tooling change is small.
- Bent tip is acceptable. Note it in the quick start card: "push, don't hammer".
