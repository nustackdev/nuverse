ASA cap, silicone gasket (Shore 40), PA6 cable gland on the probe lead.

## Tests
- IP67, 30 min at 1 m: 10 of 10 pass.
- UV, 500 h: ASA color shift slight, no chalking. The ABS control went yellow and brittle at the edges.
- Antenna detuning from the new cap: see [[lab-antenna-tuning]].

## Pilot failure
Two pilot probes cracked at the cable gland after the first frost ([[m-pilot-retro]]). Both glands split along the mold line. PA6 here gets brittle below about -10 C once it dries out, and our glands sat in sun all summer.

## Fix
- Swap to a TPE-sealed gland, same thread. 2 cents more.
- Freeze cycles in the chamber: 20 cycles, -15 C to 20 C, wet. Booked after the battery run ([[lab-cold-chamber]]).
- Drops with the new gland in [[lab-drop-test]].
